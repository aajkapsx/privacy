# Privacy Policy: Pakistan Gold & Silver Rates

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy covers the Facebook Page "Pakistan Gold & Silver Rates" and the small tool that posts daily gold and silver rates to it.

**What we do.** The tool collects public gold and silver rates and posts them to our Facebook Page as a picture and caption.

**What we collect.** We do not collect, store or sell personal information about visitors or followers. The tool only uses the Page's own access to publish posts.

**Third parties.** Posts are published through Facebook (Meta). Facebook's own privacy policy applies to how Facebook handles your data.

**Rates are for information only.** Please confirm rates with your local jeweller before buying or selling.

**Contact.** For any questions, message the Facebook Page "Pakistan Gold & Silver Rates".
